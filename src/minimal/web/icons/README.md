# Provider marks

These SVGs are served locally; opening the dashboard makes no logo requests to vendor websites. Retrieved on 2026-10-06 from primary sources:

- `codex.svg`: OpenAI's company mark for Codex, extracted from [the official OpenAI website favicon](https://openai.com/favicon.svg). The glyph geometry is unchanged; the favicon background is removed, the viewBox uses the original glyph clipping bounds, and the fill is white for the dark dashboard.
- `claude.svg`: Claude's orange symbol from [the official website favicon](https://claude.com/favicon.svg), unchanged.
- `antigravity.svg`: [Google Antigravity press kit](https://antigravity.google/press), [full-color SVG](https://antigravity.google/assets/image/brand/antigravity-icon__full-color.svg), unchanged.

The marks identify their respective providers. They remain their owners' trademarks and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.
